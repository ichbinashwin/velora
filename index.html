FAQ

Ladies wear manufacturing: frequently asked questions

What does Velora Apparel manufacture?

Velora Apparel manufactures ladies wear in Alindra, Gujarat, India: kurtis, co-ord sets, tops, office wear, night wear and dresses, for brands, boutiques, resellers and private labels.

Does Velora Apparel offer OEM and private label manufacturing?

Yes. Velora Apparel offers OEM manufacturing, private label manufacturing, job work and bulk production for ladies wear.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

The minimum order quantity depends on the product, fabric and design. Share your target quantity per design and Velora Apparel will confirm what is possible for your order.

How long does ladies wear production take?

Timelines depend on quantity, fabric availability and design. Bulk production starts after sample approval, and a practical delivery timeline is agreed with you before work begins.

Do you make samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sampling is part of every order, and bulk production begins only after you approve the sample.

Can I supply my own fabric?

Yes. With job work you supply the fabric and Velora Apparel handles cutting, stitching, quality checking and packing. Fabric selection support is also part of sampling.

Do you work with boutiques and new clothing brands?

Yes. Velora Apparel works with boutiques, resellers, private label brands and established clothing brands.

How much does it cost to manufacture kurtis or ladies wear?

Pricing depends on fabric, design, quantity and finishing. Send your product details on WhatsApp or by email to receive a quote.

Where is Velora Apparel located?

Velora Apparel is based in the village of Alindra, Gujarat, India. The owner is Rajanikant Dhirajlal Parmar.

What is Velora Apparel's production process?

Production follows eight steps: consultation, sampling, fabric selection, cutting, stitching, quality checking, packing and delivery.

What information is needed for a production quote?

Share your product category, target quantity, fabric preference, size range and delivery timeline. Velora Apparel then replies with the next steps for sampling and production.

How can I contact Velora Apparel?

Call or WhatsApp +91 79909 65209, or email rajani.parmar.tailor@gmail.com.
