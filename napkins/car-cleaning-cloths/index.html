FAQ

Microfiber Car Cleaning Cloths: questions and answers

Why use microfiber for car cleaning?

Microfiber's very fine fibres trap dust and absorb water, so it cleans glass and paint without leaving lint.

How do I wash microfiber car cloths?

Wash in cool or warm water with a mild detergent, without fabric softener or bleach, and air dry. Fabric softener clogs the fibres and reduces cleaning power.
