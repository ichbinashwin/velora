Multipurpose Cleaning Cloths: questions and answers

What is colour-coded cleaning?

Using a different cloth colour for each area, for example one colour for the kitchen and another for the bathroom, so cloths are not mixed between areas. Assorted colour packs make this easy.

Microfiber or cotton for dusting?

Microfiber traps dust instead of spreading it and leaves no lint, so it is better for dusting and glass. Cotton is good for general wiping.
