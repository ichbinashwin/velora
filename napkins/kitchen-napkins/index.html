Kitchen Napkins & Towels: questions and answers

Which material is best for kitchen napkins?

Terry cotton is the most absorbent for drying dishes and hands. Waffle cotton is light and dries quickly. Plain cotton is soft and works well for covering rotis and food.

Are the kitchen napkins washable and reusable?

Yes. Cotton, terry and waffle kitchen napkins can be washed and reused. Wash them before first use; new cotton becomes more absorbent after a few washes.
